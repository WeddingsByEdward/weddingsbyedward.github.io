about us

Wedding décor, made personal

Weddings by Edward designs wedding décor for Bengaluru couples and their families. Since 2018 we have set mandaps, stages and florals for muhurthams, nikahs, church weddings, roce ceremonies, engagements and receptions.

We begin by listening. Your traditions, your venue and the feeling you want your guests to carry home guide every choice, from the frame of the stage to the last flower. We are honest about budgets, and we take care of the details so you can enjoy your day.

meet the founder

Edward Jenner

Founder · Visual designer

Weddings by Edward was founded by Edward Jenner, a professional visual designer. That design training shapes every wedding we set: how colour, light and flowers work together, how a stage looks from the last row, and how it photographs from every angle.

It means more creative setups and real attention to detail. Every mandap, stage and entrance is drawn up with care before a single flower is placed, so your décor feels considered, balanced and completely yours.
